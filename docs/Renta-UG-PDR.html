About this document

It describes the system as built: what each part does, the rules it follows and how to run it. The pictures are from the app build, run locally. Pages inside the app are described in sections 5 to 8.

1Summary and goals

Renta UG is a rent management app for Uganda. Landlords keep their houses, rentals, rooms, tenants and rent in one clear account. Tenants pay from their phone with MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, straight to the landlord, and always know how many days they have left. Renta UG (the main admin) runs the platform and earns from landlord subscriptions.

Convenience

Pay and confirm rent from a phone, any time. No middleman, no paperwork.

Security

Each person sees only their own data; money records are written only by checked database rules.

Accuracy

One set of rules for time and money, shared by the app and the database and tested on every deploy.

Goals

  • Replace notebooks and receipts books with one shared, trusted record between landlord and tenant.
  • Make paying rent simple on any phone, including part payments, without Renta UG holding money.
  • Show everyone, at a glance, who has paid and who has not.
  • Give the platform owner full oversight: landlords, subscriptions, activity, health.

What Renta UG is not

  • It does not collect, hold or move tenants' money: rent goes directly to the landlord's line or in cash.
  • It does not set or guarantee rent; landlords set rents and confirm what they receive.

4Signing up and signing in

Tenant sign-up

  1. Enter full name, email and a password (at least 8 characters).
  2. Renta UG emails a 6-character code, valid 20 minutes (check Spam/Promotions).
  3. Enter the code: the account is created only now, so nobody can register an email they don't own.

First sign-in (landlords and tenants given app access)

  1. Type the email or phone number and tap Continue: no code and no temporary password.
  2. Create your own password. Landlords go straight on to the business-size page.

Rules for everyone

  • The page recognises the kind of account as you type (landlord, tenant, first sign-in, suspended, not found).
  • Phone numbers match on the last 9 digits: 0772 123456 = +256 772 123456.
  • Wrong passwords on phone sign-in are counted and the account is locked for a while after too many.
  • Forgot password: a reset link is emailed. A sign-in from a new device sends a security alert.
  • The main admin signs in on a separate page, /admin2026.

5Landlord: setup and subscription

There is no free trial. A landlord uses Renta UG once a subscription payment is confirmed. Until then the setup runs full screen, with only Help, Inbox and Sign out at the top.

  1. Business size: "How big is your rental business?" A large number box with − / + and quick choices (1–5, up to 10, 20, 50, 100+). A card shows the matching package and its monthly price live.
  2. Package: Small or Big scale, monthly or yearly (yearly shows the saving). Small is greyed out when the number is above the limit; a "Change" link returns to the business size.
  3. Line to pay to: Renta UG's MTN or Airtel number and the registered name.
  4. Send the money: number and amount with Copy buttons, numbered steps and a Dial button.
  5. Confirm: the full names registered on the line used (at least two names), and a tick box.
  6. Waiting: "Waiting for confirmation" with the details, Check now and Withdraw. When Renta UG confirms, a "Payment confirmed" screen opens the dashboard.

Subscription page (after paying)

  • Status card: package, "Active until …", days left and a meter; houses and rooms used out of the limit.
  • Paid subscription: amount, date, method, timeline from start to end, days left.
  • Control panel: renew early (time is added on top), change package, billing history, refresh status, houses and rooms, contact Renta UG (opens WhatsApp).
  • Billing history: every payment with status (Waiting, Paid, Not confirmed, Withdrawn) and the dates it covers.

Rules

  • Reminders 7, 3 and 1 days before the end, on the day and the day after. When it ends, the account locks (data is kept) until renewed.
  • A Small subscriber can't add houses or rooms beyond the limit; the database refuses it too.
  • Renta UG can record a subscription paid in cash, or add free days.

The pay flow in detail

  1. Choose the line the landlord added (MTN or Airtel).
  2. How much: type any amount (at least UGX 500) or tap Everything owed / One month / Half. A card shows "UGX 150,000 lasts 15 days, up to 5 Dec", any debt days and how much more completes the month.
  3. Send: the instructions repeat "You said you will pay UGX …, it lasts N days", the number and amount to copy, the dial code.
  4. Confirm: the full names registered on the line used, and a tick box.
  5. Waiting: what happens next and "+N days, to [date]" once confirmed.

Complete payment (a part-paid month) opens the same flow showing the remaining amount, the progress and a box for the amount the tenant managed to top up.

  1. Open the landlord (or Subscriptions → Paid cash).

9Money and time rules

Days = amount paid ÷ monthly rent × 30

rounded down to whole days

Rental time

The period a tenant's rent covers (lease_start → lease_end). Only the database sets it, when money is confirmed. Nobody types dates.
  • A rent month is always 30 days (never 28, 29 or 31).
  • Part payments buy days: days = money paid on that month ÷ the month's rent × 30, rounded down.
  • A month paid in full gives exactly 30 days in total; days already given are never counted twice.
  • First payment: the rental time starts on the day it was paid.
  • Late payment: time continues from the old end; the days in between are debt days and are not given back.
  • Money is used on unpaid months first (oldest first), then on months ahead (up to 24).
  • Months that counted under the earlier rule (a whole month at 60%) keep their month.

Rent months in the database

Each rent month (a row in payments) records its rent, the amount paid, the days it has already given (credited_days) and whether it has given its whole month (month_credited). A database trigger extends the tenant's rental time every time money is added.

11Payments step by step

Mobile money from a tenant

  1. The tenant sends money to the landlord's line and submits it with the sender names.
  2. It waits as a payment to confirm (at most 3 waiting per tenant). The tenant is told "Payment being reviewed".
  3. The landlord sees an amber box "Name says they paid UGX …" with method, date and names, and an Amount you actually received box (can be corrected; "Same as claimed" resets it).
  4. A preview shows the days it covers. Confirm → unpaid months first, then months ahead; receipts are created; the tenant is notified; rental time grows.
  5. Or Reject with a reason: the tenant is told.

Cash

  • A tenant can report cash; the landlord confirms it as above.
  • A landlord records cash (any tenant) or mobile money (managed tenants): it counts at once, with the date it was paid.

Completing a part payment

  • Tenant: Payments → Complete your payment → line → remaining amount and top-up box → send → confirm → waiting.
  • Landlord: tenant page → Complete payment → Record the rest (pre-filled), or a month's details → Record the rest.

Checks the database makes

  • Amount above zero; date not in the future nor more than a year ago.
  • Not more than 24 months ahead; extra money needs a monthly rent.
  • Mobile money needs the landlord's number for that network and the sender names.
  • Only the tenant's landlord can confirm or reject.

12Receipts

  • Every confirmed payment has a receipt image (PNG, landscape) for tenants and landlords.
  • File name: the tenant's name and receipt number, e.g. John Kamera Receipt No. 30591874.png. The 8-digit number is always the same for the same payment.
  • Contents: amount, received from, phone, property, landlord, rent month(s), method, date paid, confirmed on, confirmed by, details.
  • Paid in turns: a table of every instalment (number, date, method, amount, days covered), the instalment the receipt is for highlighted, and "Paid X of Y · Balance Z · N of 30 days covered". A part-paid month is marked "PART PAYMENT · CONFIRMED".
  • Where: tenant Payments (payments and month details); landlord Transactions, Payments history and month details.

15Security and privacy

Row-level security

Every table checks who is asking. Landlords reach only their tenants; tenants only themselves; the admin reads everything.

Browser write limits

Payments, receipts and rental time are written only by database functions. Tenant rows accept only the fields the app edits. Notices are plain (1–1,000 characters): no fake confirmations. Sizes are limited.

Checked money actions

Confirming, recording and subscriptions run in database functions that check the caller and every amount and date.

Code and keys

Only minified code is published, with no source maps. Secret keys live only in server functions, never in the browser.

Browser protection

Strict Content-Security-Policy (no inline or outside scripts), HTTPS only, no framing, no type sniffing, limited browser permissions.

Accounts

Passwords stored only scrambled; emailed sign-up codes; new-device alerts; rate-limited phone sign-in; the permanent account is protected in the database.

Any website's code is downloaded by the browser that runs it. What protects Renta UG is that the code holds no secrets and the database refuses everything a person is not allowed to do.

Privacy

  • Data held: account details, tenancy and rent records, payment details, notifications, technical logs.
  • Kept while the account is open; payment records as long as needed for accounts and the law (up to 7 years); error logs up to 12 months.
  • Account deletion on request (Delete account page), normally within 30 days.

19Deploying on Netlify

  1. Run database/update_existing.sql in the Supabase SQL Editor.
  2. In Netlify → Site configuration → Environment variables, set every key below.
  3. Push to the repository. Netlify runs npm test && npm run build on Node 22 and publishes dist/; a failing test stops the deploy and the live site stays as it was.

20Testing and quality

  • Automated tests (tests/), run before every deploy:
    • first sign-in needs no code; wrong passwords are rate-limited;
    • a rent month is always 30 days;
    • part payments buy days; half + the rest = exactly 30 days;
    • first payment, months ahead, debt days, money already waiting;
    • months credited under the old rule give no extra days;
    • Paid / Part paid / Not paid;
    • "x days left" wording; receipt numbers and file names.
  • Build checks: every page compiles; every name a page uses is defined; every server function loads.
  • In the running app: errors from any page are reported to the admin (grouped), and the system checks itself every 20 minutes.

22Known limits and next steps

  • Payments are confirmed by people (landlord, Renta UG), not read automatically from MTN/Airtel; a future version could connect to the networks' payment services.
  • Calendar-labelled rent months (e.g. "December rent") are labels; the time they buy is always 30 days.
  • Possible additions: printable statements per tenant, several staff accounts per landlord, scheduled notices, more languages (Luganda, Runyankole).
